    Slide notes (ProPresenter, for the confidence monitor)

      How Porch Slides works

      1. Load the requests. Load the week's requests .txt with the button, drop it on the page, paste the text with Paste text, or open a link with the requests in it. Each line is one request, like Slide 2: Mark 9:9-13 (ESV), a point, or Author – "Quote". Lines starting with > under a reference give that passage's verse text.

      2. Scripture, the Porch way. Each verse read gets its own slide, showing a block of verses filled to nine lines. The verse being read is at full strength and the verses around it are dimmed. The block stays put while the highlight moves down it. Psalms, Proverbs, Job, Song of Solomon and Lamentations are set in their poetry lines, flush left.

      3. Look over the warnings. Anything to check (verse text from the request file, verses that still need text, text too tall for the box) is listed on the left and badged on each slide.

      4. Edit. In the grid, click a slide to select it, move with the arrow keys, drag to reorder, press Delete to remove it (⌘Z brings it back), and double-click or press Enter to open it. In the editor, click the text to type right on the slide, as in ProPresenter. Enter adds a line break, and Option+Enter splits the slide at the cursor. Esc goes back to the grid. Select words and press Dim / undim (or ⌘D) to dim or undim them. The context buttons add or remove a verse above or below, and the translation picker switches the whole passage. Bolls doesn't carry the NIV, so NIV verses are pasted in or typed.

      5. Export. ProPresenter bundle makes a .probundle with live, editable text on the theme's own slides, the five intro blanks and a closing blank. The week's Porch Live locations go on the third intro slide, as text (one per line) and in its notes, for the confidence monitor. They come from the # locations: line the PCO listener on Pro 3 adds to the request file from that week's Live Locations task in Asana, and you can type over them. Double-click it on the Pro machine to import. PNGs (transparent, 3840×2160) are there as a backup.

      Review link copies a link with the whole deck in it, for a communicator or producer to check. Proof sheet is a printable list of every slide.
